About M.E.S. Polytechnic

Excellence in Technical Education Since Decades

M.E.S. Polytechnic, Madhugiri (Institution Code 347), managed by Madhugiri Education Society (R), is recognized by the Government of Karnataka and approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi. Located in Madhugiri, Tumkur District, the institute is a premier destination for diploma engineering aspirants.
