About M.E.S. Polytechnic

Madhugiri Education Society (R) • Recognized by Govt. of Karnataka & Approved by AICTE, New Delhi • Code 347

Our Institutional Vision

To emerge as a premier technical education institution in Karnataka, fostering innovation, domain expertise, and high moral standards among diploma engineers, thereby contributing to state and national industrial development.

Our Mission

  • Provide state-of-the-art laboratory infrastructure and hands-on industrial training.
  • Implement outcome-based C25 curriculum with continuous evaluation and mentorship.
  • Empower students from all socio-economic backgrounds with professional career opportunities.
